# RICKSHAW RUSH: LAST STOP LEGEND
## BRAND IDENTITY & VISUAL STYLE GUIDE

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Phase:** 5 (Publishing Assets & Store Preparation)  
**Primary Mascot / Subject:** The Classic Kerala Auto-Rickshaw (*Tuk-Tuk*)  
**Tagline:** *PICK UP. DODGE. DELIVER. REPEAT.*  

---

## 1. BRAND ESSENCE & ART STYLE

*Rickshaw Rush: Last Stop Legend* combines arcade twitch-reflex endless running with an authentic, vibrant celebration of Kerala, India. The visual style is **stylized arcade cartoon**:
- High-saturation tropical palettes (emerald greens, warm golden yellows, azure waterway blues).
- Clean vector geometry, bold outlines, and 3D extruded arcade typography.
- Authentic cultural motifs: traditional Kerala tiled houses (*naalukettu*), coconut palm trees (*thengu*), backwater houseboats (*kettuvallam*), Chinese fishing nets (*cheena vala*), and mist-covered Munnar tea hills.
- Zero copyrighted vehicle badges, logos, or commercial branding.

## 3. TYPOGRAPHY SYSTEM

- **Primary Logo & Title Face:** `Impact`, `'Arial Black'`, sans-serif  
  - Ultra-bold, condensed geometry suitable for arcade 3D extrusions and fast-paced mobile readability.
  - Generous letter-spacing (8px to 16px) for commanding presence.
- **Subtitles & Badges:** `'Arial Black'`, sans-serif  
  - All-caps, sharp clarity, minimum stroke width 2px for contrast against dynamic backgrounds.
- **Gameplay UI & HUD:** System Sans-Serif (`-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif`)  
  - Clean, unencumbered numbers and labels for instantaneous comprehension during high-speed traffic dodging.

---

## 4. LOGO CLEAR SPACE & SCALING RULES

1. **Clear Space:** Maintain a minimum clear space surrounding the logo equal to 25% of the logo height. No foreign text or interface elements should invade this safety zone.
2. **Minimum Reproducible Sizes:**
   - Vector Logo: 1920x600 px (Master), 480x150 px (Minimum web header).
   - Square App Icon: 512x512 px (Store Master), 180x180 px (Mobile Springboard), 64x64 px (Favicon).
3. **Integrity Rules:**
   - **DO NOT** stretch, condense, or alter the aspect ratio of the logo.
   - **DO NOT** place third-party platform logos (Poki, CrazyGames, YouTube) directly onto game artwork.
   - **DO NOT** apply realistic photo textures or copyrighted auto-maker emblems (Bajaj, Piaggio, TVS).
   - **DO NOT** use real-money currency symbols (`₹`, `$`) for in-game arcade coins.
