HOW TO PLAY:

1. LANE SWITCHING:
   • Steer your auto-rickshaw across the 3-lane road to dodge oncoming Kerala buses, taxis, cars, and wandering cows.

2. PASSENGER MISSIONS (UNIQUE MECHANIC):
   • Watch for roadside passenger stands on the road shoulder.
   • Steer into the station and slow down to board the waiting passenger.
   • Race against the distance counter to reach their destination drop-off bay.
   • Pull into the glowing destination zone to collect massive fare coin bonuses!

3. HORN & COWS:
   • When a wandering cow blocks your lane, press the HORN button or tap the HORN zone to scare it away into the road shoulder.

4. POWER-UPS & COMBOS:
   • CHAYA SHIELD (Blue Cup): Protects your rickshaw against one traffic collision.
   • COIN MAGNET (Red Horseshoe): Automatically draws all nearby coins into your vehicle.
   • NITRO BOOST (Yellow Lightning): Grants explosive invulnerable speed through heavy traffic.
   • NEAR MISSES: Pass closely next to traffic vehicles without touching them to trigger near-miss rewards and combo score multipliers up to 5x!

5. MODES:
   • Choose NORMAL DRIVE for bright tropical sunshine, or switch to MONSOON DRIVE on the Start Menu for an authentic Kerala rain experience with rain particles, roadside puddles, and distant thunder!
