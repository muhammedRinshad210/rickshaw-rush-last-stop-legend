GAME CONTROLS:

MOBILE / TOUCH DEVICES:
• Swipe Left / Right: Switch traffic lanes.
• Tap Left Half / Right Half: Steer left or right.
• Tap Horn Button (Bottom Right): Honk dual-tone auto horn to scare cows.
• Tap Brake Zone (Bottom Center): Slow down for passenger boarding and tight traffic gaps.
• Tap Pause Button (Top Right): Pause gameplay and open options.

KEYBOARD (DESKTOP):
• A / Left Arrow: Steer Left
• D / Right Arrow: Steer Right
• W / Up Arrow: Accelerate / Resume Normal Speed
• S / Down Arrow: Brake / Slow Down for Passenger Stations
• Spacebar / H: Honk Horn (Scares cows)
• P / Escape: Pause / Resume Game

MOUSE:
• Click Left / Right Side of Screen: Change lanes
• Click On-Screen Horn Button: Honk Horn
• Click On-Screen Brake Button: Brake
• Click UI Buttons: Navigate menus and garage
