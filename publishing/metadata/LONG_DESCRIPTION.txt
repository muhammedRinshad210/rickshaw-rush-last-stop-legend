Take the wheel in Rickshaw Rush: Last Stop Legend! Drive through the vibrant, bustling roads of Kerala, dodge chaotic traffic, collect shiny arcade coins, pick up eccentric passengers, and complete exciting delivery missions before the timer runs out!

Embark on a breathtaking Kerala road trip spanning five iconic locations:
• Kerala Village (0-45s): Cruise past lush green paddy fields, banana groves, rustic tea stalls, and traditional red-tiled village homes.
• Alappuzha Backwaters (45-90s): Navigate scenic bridges overlooking deep azure canals and traditional thatched houseboats (kettuvallam).
• Cherai Beach Road (90-135s): Feel the ocean breeze beside the Arabian Sea, animated waves, a classic lighthouse, and historic Chinese fishing nets.
• Munnar Tea Hills (135-180s): Climb winding highland roads flanked by rolling emerald tea bushes and mountain mist.
• Kochi Town at Night (180s+): Test your reflexes in an endless nighttime rush through glowing neon storefronts, street lamps, and illuminated city traffic!

KEY HIGHLIGHTS:
• Unique Passenger Delivery Mechanic: Pull safely into glowing roadside stations to pick up students, office commuters, tourists, and grandmas, then drop them at their destination for massive coin bonuses!
• Kerala Monsoon Mode: Toggle between sunny Normal Drive and atmospheric Monsoon Drive featuring real-time rainfall, road puddles, distant thunder, and post-storm rainbows—with 100% fair handling!
• Dual-Tone Auto Horn: Honk your horn to clear stubborn wandering cows from the roadway.
• Skill-Based Near-Miss System: Skim past oncoming buses, taxis, and lorries to build up combo multipliers and earn floating arcade accolades!
• Exciting Arcade Power-Ups: Grab the Chaya Shield for invincibility, the Coin Magnet to vacuum up nearby riches, and the Nitro Boost for high-speed invulnerable cruising.
• Rickshaw Garage: Unlock 5 authentic vehicle designs using earned in-game coins, including the Champion Gold, Malabar Red, Backwater Blue, and Racing Green!
• 100% Free Arcade Action: Pure responsive HTML5 canvas gameplay designed for mobile, tablet, and desktop with zero downloads required!
